## Verified

- **Leading coefficient.** The jerk is $\epsilon\nabla(\Delta K+2\nabla\log g\cdot\nabla K)/2$ at $x=q$. This reduces to $\epsilon(D^2K(0))(-q)=\epsilon Hq$. The dilation derivative $2s\,e^{-1/2}/\sqrt\pi=2\phi(1)$ is correct. $D-1=1.25375\times10^{-40}$ and $6.06744\times10^{-41}$ are correct. The SI-unit restoration ($Gm\omega H/a^3$, $m$, $\ell$, $T$) is consistent.
- **Field representation.** The radial measure is correct: $M_0=2/\sqrt\pi=K(0)$ and $M_2/3=H$. The tilted bounds 2, 3, 7, 21, 80, 330 hold. The field moments $8=M_0+2M_1$, $17$, $49$ hold. The $|B-z(0)|\le2kL_t$ bound and the retarded sine form are correct.
- **Coupling and tail.** $\delta=C_Rt^4/4$ and $e_R<1.64\times10^{-42}$ hold. The tail is $b_{16}\approx1.2\times10^{-110}$. The choice $R=\sqrt{6\log(1/t)}$ gives $e_R=o(t^3)$.
- **Statistics.** The separation is $0.45(D-1)-2e_*-8\times10^{-43}\approx5.2\times10^{-41}>5\times10^{-41}$. The Hoeffding count is $1.40\times10^{82}$. The χ²/Pinsker bound gives $0.81\cdot0.42/(7\times10^{-41})^2=6.94\times10^{79}$. The simple-hypothesis restriction is legitimate, because zero offsets lie inside the composite class.

## Issues

1. **Definition of $P_\epsilon(t)$ in Theorem 2 (moderate, clarity/rigor).** Existence is proved only for $|q|\le R$ with $(R+1)t\le0.01$. At any fixed $t$ the tail dynamics are unresolved, so $P_\epsilon(t)$ is not a defined number. The phrase "any measurable assignment of bits on the unresolved tail" carries the definition informally. *Requested:* define $P_\epsilon(t)$ explicitly as any value in the interval generated by tail conventions, or use outer and inner probabilities. Then state the theorem as uniform over that set.

2. **Measurability of $q\mapsto Q(t;q)$ (minor).** The bit is measurable on the ball only if the solution map is measurable. The ingredients are already present: Lipschitz dependence on the evaluation point and the path, plus $q$-dependence of the static prehistory through $z(0)$. *Requested:* add one Gronwall sentence giving continuity in $q$. Also state the continuation argument explicitly: the bootstrap set is closed, and $F\neq0$ in the tube excludes nodes.

3. **Proposition 1 is formal (minor).** It differentiates the wave and phase equations without establishing $C^3$ regularity of $Q$ at $0^+$. The rigorous content is the path estimate, which yields only a Peano-type third-order coefficient. *Requested:* restate Proposition 1 as that coefficient, derived as a corollary of the path estimate, or prove the needed smoothness.

4. **Unverifiable constants (minor, reproducibility).** The one-kick bound, the two-kick moments, and the "$<600t^3$" marked-kick bound are asserted without derivation. The sum over orders $n\ge3$ in the marked estimate is also only implicit. $C_R$ has roughly a 200-fold margin at $R=16$, so the claims are robust. Still, give an appendix or a pinned repository commit with the derivation and code.

5. **Notation (minor, clarity).** "Imaginary coupling" is $\epsilon$, but $\epsilon$ is the *real* part of $\alpha=i+\epsilon$. Map this explicitly to Laloë's notation. Theorem 2 also says "difference of ideal probabilities" when it means actual probabilities. The temperature figure is closer to $7.7\times10^{-17}$ K than to the stated 7.6.

## Limits (correctly disclosed; keep them prominent)

- The readout is a contract, not a derived detector model.
- The lower bound covers only independent reads of this bit at this time.
- A differential drift of about $10^{-40}$ can erase the effect. The abstract should keep the word "conditional."

**Release recommendation:** Fix items 1–3 and post. Items 4 and 5 are strongly encouraged.
