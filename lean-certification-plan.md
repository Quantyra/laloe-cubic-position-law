# Lean certification plan

**Certification status: planned.** Use the [reviewed manuscript snapshot](https://github.com/Quantyra/laloe-cubic-position-law/blob/ce117ec5af78ff19d30c63963fdd7a88157c5e5d/manuscript.md) and its [Arb replay](https://github.com/Quantyra/laloe-cubic-position-law/blob/ce117ec5af78ff19d30c63963fdd7a88157c5e5d/verify.py) as the claim map and numerical evidence. No Lean theorem is claimed here. A proof can cover the probability calculation conditional on a trajectory estimate before a separate proof derives that estimate from the dynamics.

## Statement and interfaces

Use oscillator time $t=\omega T$, source width $a=\ell$, coupling $\lambda=1$, $0\le\epsilon\le 10^{-3}$, positive finite $\nu=c/(\omega\ell)$ and the two arms $\epsilon$ and zero. Use real Euclidean three-space, the law $\pi^{-3/2}e^{-|q|^2}dq$, a fixed unit axis $e$, and $s=1/\sqrt2$. Initial configurations have that law even though each source history has a different wave. Static histories extend through $t=0$ in the main theorem. Keep the SI example and the bounded-variation history extension as separate corollaries.

The ideal bit is $\mathbf 1_{\{|e\cdot Q_\epsilon(t;q)|>s\}}$. On the unresolved tail allow any *measurable* assignment to 0 or 1, including different assignments in the two arms. Cover the short-time coefficient for every such completion. The finite-time statistic is a difference from an independently prepared time-zero baseline, with the same stationary nuisance law at both times in each model. Use independent Bernoulli records only in the sampling claim.

## Proof order and acceptance checks

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance and countercheck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Events and transport | From a measurable trajectory on the retained ball and a path error $\delta$, bound the change of the strict two-sided event under dilation by $4\delta/\sqrt\pi+b_R$. Prove the band inclusions, marginal density bound, and 3D radial Gaussian tail formula. | Take the supremum over both exterior bit assignments. Handle equality at the thresholds. A final-time Born-law assumption invalidates the statement. |
| B. Asymptotic law | Differentiate the dilated initial tail probability. With $R(t)=\sqrt{6\log(1/t)}$, establish admissibility and the error $o(t^3)$. Match the coefficient $2\phi(1)\epsilon H/6$ with $H=4/(3\sqrt\pi)$. | Set $\epsilon=0$, then check both tails and the positive sign. If the trajectory bound is a hypothesis, report stages A–B as conditional. |
| C. Retarded potential | Transform the spatially retarded Gaussian density to the forced oscillator for each Fourier mode. Prove the integration-by-parts formula, the $2kL_t$ bound and the $k\eta$ extension for nonconstant history. | Include Fourier normalization, the $k=0$ limit, static past data, finite light speed and the sign of the imaginary coupling. An asserted kernel multiplier leaves the transform open. |
| D. Pathwise dynamics | For a prescribed path justify the coherent-state expansion of the harmonic propagator, ordered integrals, spatial derivatives, moments, and the node-free ball. Establish existence, continuation, uniqueness and measurable dependence on the starting point for the coupled delay-guidance equation. | Use the same path to generate the potential and sample the phase gradient. Local results on the ball suffice with A. A prescribed external source does not discharge the feedback step. |
| E. Fourth-order path bound | Prove the one-kick coefficient, two-kick parity cancellation, marked-factor estimate and denominator bound. Combine them for both arms into $|Q(t;q)-(1+\epsilon Ht^3/6)q|\le 10^4(1+R)^2t^4/4$. | Track the norm of complex gradients, the time-simplex factors, $R$ dependence, and exceptional tail. Reconnect this declaration to A–B without assuming the desired cubic law. |
| F. Finite example and sampling | Validate the Gaussian moments and tails with directed/rational bounds, including the bounded-history correction. Derive the shifted-threshold slopes, failure budgets, and the disjoint increment intervals. Prove the Hoeffding upper allocation and Bernoulli relative entropy/Pinsker lower bound at the selected time. | Keep trial independence, matched timing and the 5% error criterion explicit. Distinguish the per-time count from total preparations. Numerical intervals from Python are test vectors; Lean needs its own proved enclosures. |

## First bounded allocation

Proposition 1 needs the right-hand limit of the normalized displacement for each fixed starting point. Derive it from stage E using a fixed admissible radius. A classical third time derivative would be a stronger target. For Theorem 2 instead let the retained radius grow with decreasing time and prove a uniform error for every selection from each arm's interval, independently. Include Borel measurability of the retained solution map among D's exports.
